## Why Enterprises Need Agentic Governance (6 Risks)

Security & Data Risks:
- Data Leakage: agents can expose sensitive data to unauthorized systems
- Uncontrolled Actions: autonomous decisions carry financial and legal consequences

Compliance & Accuracy Risks:
- Compliance Failures: violations of GDPR, HIPAA, SOC 2, and industry regulations (BACEN, LGPD in Brasil)
- Hallucinated Execution: confident but incorrect outputs drive business decisions

Organizational Risks:
- Organizational Disruption: unclear human-agent workflows create chaos
- Lack of Auditability: agent decisions cannot be traced or investigated
