## 1. BUSINESS MODEL AND SCALE

### What DATAPREV Does
**[Core Mission]** — DATAPREV (Empresa de Tecnologia e Informações da Previdência Social) is Brazil's federal technology and information company for social security, operating for 51 years.

**Core Services:**
- Social security system technology infrastructure (historically for INSS)
- Payroll lending systems (largest revenue source at 60% of total)
- Pension management solutions (COMPREV)
- E-commerce platform for federal debt collection ("Comprei")
- Cloud/Desktop-as-a-Service (DaaS, multinuvem)
- Blockchain services
- Data center operations (TIER III certified, CEEDA energy efficiency)

Source: `https://www.dataprev.gov.br` | Confidence: High

**[Customer Base]** — Serves 25+ distinct government entities across municipal pension institutes, judicial bodies, federal/state agencies, and financial institutions for INSS consignment services.

Customer types include:
- INSS (National Social Security Institute) - historical core client (19.2% of revenue in 2025)
- Municipal pension funds (IPREJI, PREV QUEIMADOS, JFPREV, others)
- Judicial bodies (TJDFT, TCMSP, CJF)
- Federal entities (Pré-Sal Petróleo PPSA)
- Financial institutions (SICOOB, Midway, Novo Banco Continental)
- Research centers (CPQD)

Source: `https://www.dataprev.gov.br/acesso-informacao/contratos-com-clientes` (20 of 124 pages) | Confidence: High

**[Financial Performance FY 2025]** — Net profit R$ 900 million (77% growth); payroll lending became largest revenue source at 60%, INSS services 19.2%.

Revenue breakdown:
- Payroll lending ("consignado"): 60% of revenue
- INSS services: 19.2% of revenue
- E-commerce platform "Comprei": R$ 3.6 billion in transactions (169% growth), 6.16M visits, 14,800 buyers

Source: `https://www.convergenciadigital.com.br` (FY 2025 results reported April 2026) | Confidence: High

**[Employee Count]** — Opening 212 immediate positions + 1,611 reserve positions (July 2026) for IT analysts, developers, cybersecurity; starting salary R$ 10,600/month.

Note: User context suggests ~3,000 employees total (not directly confirmed in public sources).

Source: `https://www.convergenciadigital.com.br` (July 2026 hiring announcement) | Confidence: High

**[Organizational Structure]** — 6 main directorates plus Presidency: Technology & Operations (DIT), Relationship & Business (DRN), Finance & Logistics (DFL), ICT Infrastructure (DGI), Products & Solutions (DPS), Management & Governance (DGG).

Source: `https://www.dataprev.gov.br/conheca-dataprev-quem-e-quem/organograma` | Confidence: High

## 2. RECENT STRATEGIC INITIATIVES (2023-2026)

**[Mainframe Decommissioning - January 2026]** — Completed historic shutdown of last mainframe used for INSS systems (Jan 28-30, 2026), third and final mainframe decommissioned; 220+ IT professionals involved in migration.

Key details:
- Complete transition from legacy mainframe to modern platforms
- Temporary service slowdowns during migration due to high biometric authentication access
- Fiber optic cable break on January 27 caused separate instabilities
- Aimed at improving scalability and service delivery

Source: `https://www.convergenciadigital.com.br` (January-February 2026 coverage) | Confidence: High

**[AI Partnership with Huawei - February 2026]** — DATAPREV partnered with Huawei to map artificial intelligence demands across Northeast Brazil.

Focus areas:
- Chatbots for public services
- Medical records management systems
- Monitoring systems

Source: `https://www.convergenciadigital.com.br` (February 2026) | Confidence: High

**[Worker Credit Program Integration - April 2026]** — SERPRO system integrated eSocial, DATAPREV, and Caixa databases; managed R$ 120 billion in payroll lending across 21.6M active contracts serving 9.5M beneficiaries.

Source: `https://www.convergenciadigital.com.br` (April 2026) | Confidence: High

**[Strategic Planning Framework]** — Institutional Strategic Plan (PEI) covers 5-year periods; current plan 2026-2030; follows State-Owned Companies Law (Lei 13.303/2016).

Planning includes:
- Mission and vision revision
- Strategic objectives and indicators
- Corporate risk identification
- Corporate program portfolio
- Value chain review

Source: `https://www.dataprev.gov.br/gestao-estrategica/planejamento-estrategico-institucional` | Confidence: High

## 5. KEY CHALLENGES

**[Service Disruptions]** — Meu INSS app experienced failures; scheduled shutdown Jan 27-31, 2026 for technological updates; TCU demanded action on eSocial system failures (May 2025).

Details:
- Meu INSS "tem apresentado falhas" (has been experiencing failures)
- TCU gave DATAPREV 10 days to guarantee system access for inspection
- Ongoing system stability concerns under regulatory scrutiny

Source: `https://www.convergenciadigital.com.br` (January 2026, May 2025) | Confidence: High

**[Data Security Breach - May 2026]** — Meu INSS data breach expanded to 2.8 million CPF numbers exposed; 98% belonged to deceased individuals, ~52,000 living citizens affected.

Source: `https://www.convergenciadigital.com.br` (May 2026) | Confidence: High

**[Infrastructure Scalability]** — User context indicates 166× customer growth (15 to 2,500 B2B customers) without infrastructure evolution; managing ~30k tickets/month.

Note: This specific metric not confirmed in public sources but aligns with observed service strain and modernization urgency.

Source: User context | Confidence: Medium (unverified in public sources)

**[Procurement/Competition Pressure]** — TCU evaluated procurement processes (December 2024), seeking more competition in data center contracts; noted ABNT standards were overly restrictive.

Source: `https://www.convergenciadigital.com.br` (December 2024) | Confidence: High

**[Workforce Volatility]** — Laid off 92 employees in April 2025, triggering reactions from employee associations; now hiring 212+ positions (July 2026).

Source: `https://www.convergenciadigital.com.br` (April 2025, July 2026) | Confidence: High

**[Financial Sustainability Risk (Historical)]** — TCU warning in March 2018: "custo operacional alto coloca Serpro e Dataprev em risco de colapso financeiro" due to high operational costs and difficulty collecting from government clients.

Note: This is historical context (2018); FY 2025 showed strong profitability (R$ 900M, 77% growth), suggesting significant turnaround.

Source: `https://www.convergenciadigital.com.br` (March 2018 coverage) | Confidence: High (historical)
